Environmental Signage

Securian Canada

As part of Securian Canada's rebrand, we redefined our brand voice and our employee value proposition: what it means to work here, and why it matters. This project brings that positioning off the page and into our office.

The messaging on the walls comes straight from our employee value proposition, so employees see what we stand for in the spaces they use every day.

Project Type

Environmental Signage | 2026

Role

Art Direction, Signage Design

Tools

Illustrator, Photoshop, InDesign

  • Challenge


    A new employee value proposition only works if people feel it. Our challenge was to move it beyond decks and onboarding documents and into the workplace itself, in a way that felt genuine rather than corporate.

  • Solution


    We translated the core EVP messages into dimensional PVC lettering and paired them with a layered ring system, mixing protruding PVC with flat vinyl, in our three brand greens. Every wall was planned on dimensioned elevations so the graphics work around doors, furniture and existing signage.

From strategy to space

Every line on these walls traces back to our employee value proposition. The graphics were designed to carry that voice: warm, confident and human.
